# Non-Goals

The Haruhi Engine was designed to fix & improve upon what many engines do wrong, alongside be a simple work station for new(er) developers, alongside a free engine for people who cannot afford the monthly or large summed payments of AAA engine.

The first non-goal Haruhi aimed to not do under **any** circumstance is cost money. Haruhi Engine is designed to be accessible for everyone & people who can not afford the large fees company charge for a service as a simple as an engine for building games.

The next major non-goal aimed was performance. Performance is a big thing across game engines, games included; although some engines are desined for quality, & that centered aspect alone, it comes at the cost of using & hogging hardware resources. Haruhi is made in a way, using the Furi (Fyur-ī) renderer built & designed for Haruhi. Performance & quality is a hard factor to balance, as when you prioritize performance, you lose quality, & vice-versa. Haruhi Engine goes in the middle (while still aimed at a quality based side), to save users' hardware & performance during development.
